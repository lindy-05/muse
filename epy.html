About Epy

Inspired by Euterpe,
muse of music

In Greek mythology, Euterpe was the muse of music, harmony and joy — the goddess musicians called upon before playing. Epy carries that spirit into your creative space.

Epy is not a metronome. It is not a tuner. It is a presence — one that listens to your playing, understands your rhythm, and offers gentle guidance when you need it most.

Whether you are rehearsing scales or composing something entirely new, Epy floats beside you and supports your creative process without ever taking control of it.
