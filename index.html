About Muse

Inspired by the
nine Muses

In ancient Greece, artists and poets called upon the Muses before creating — nine goddesses of art, science and inspiration who guided human creativity.

Today, Muse reimagines that idea through intelligent AI companions that help musicians, artists and performers grow their craft — not by replacing the human, but by being a quiet, intuitive presence that moves with the creative process.

Not a cold tool. A companion that lives in your creative space.

The story behind the names

Nine muses.
Endless inspiration.

The name Muse comes from the nine Muses of Greek mythology — the goddesses of art, science and inspiration. Each companion carries a name that connects it to its artistic discipline.

Epy is inspired by Euterpe, muse of music. Poly by Polyhymnia, muse of reflection and expression. Taly by Thalia, muse of performance and life.
